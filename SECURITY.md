# Security policy

BRAQON builds payment and point-of-sale software, so we take security reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue, discussion or pull request for security problems.**

1. Use GitHub's private reporting: open the affected repository, go to **Security → Report a vulnerability**.
2. If that is not available, email **bquintianromero@gmail.com** with the subject `SECURITY: <repository>`.

Include what you can: affected repository and version, steps to reproduce, impact, and any proof of concept.

## What to expect

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | within 3 business days |
| First assessment | within 10 business days |
| Fix or mitigation plan | depends on severity; we keep you updated |

We will credit you in the advisory unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope: code in repositories owned by [`braqon-dev`](https://github.com/braqon-dev) and packages published under [`@braqon`](https://www.npmjs.com/org/braqon) on npm.

Out of scope: third-party services, social engineering, denial of service, and findings that require a compromised device.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research that follows this policy, avoids privacy violations and service disruption, and gives us reasonable time to fix before disclosure will not be pursued legally by us.
